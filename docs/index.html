        How it works

        1. Best start

        Two common words with no shared letters, like DEALS + INTRO. Together they test the 10 letters most common in Wordle answers. Guess one, and the solver suggests the other next.

        2. Keep only the words that fit

        After each guess, a word stays possible only if guessing it would have shown exactly your colours. Double letters are handled the way Wordle does it.

        3. Likely answers

        The words that still fit, most common first. Wordle answers are everyday words, so the top of the list is usually right. Hide past drops words that have already been an answer.

        4. Test new letters

        When many lookalikes are left (BUDDY, MUDDY, CUDDY…), guessing them one by one can lose the game. These guesses rule out the most likely answers in one go, even if they can't be the answer themselves. ★ means it could also be the answer.

        Made a mistake?

        Click any tile, even in a row you already entered, to change its colour.
